[ DO ]
PI_LIME on DEEP_INK is the signature pairing. Use it for the loudest moments - buttons, indicators, the main π glyph.
[ DO ]
Pair grays for hierarchy. STEEL_BASE → FOG_GRAY → PURE_WHITE makes a chrome plate gradient.
[ DON'T ]
Don't use red anywhere. Even for "warning" states. The brand stays in lime + grays only.
[ DON'T ]
Don't put PI_LIME on a light bg without a dark plate. Lime needs dark contrast to read as "powered on."
