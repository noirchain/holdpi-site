HOW THE MODEL BECOMES SOLIDITY
All sliders compile to integer basis points. The sell and reward curves become 31 daily values. The same arrays drive this preview and initialize the contracts.
Age = max(0, floor(now / 86400) - ceil(receipt / 86400)). Rounding is conservative: a checkpoint arrives after the stated time, with less than one extra day. Settlement loops over cohorts, never every holder.
BNB is accounted for as WBNB and unwrapped on claim. Minimum funding and elapsed time must both be satisfied; anyone can trigger an eligible settlement.
